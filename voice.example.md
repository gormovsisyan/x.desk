## register — hard rules
- all lowercase. no hashtags, no emoji, no @mentions. no exclamation marks.
- one idea per post. short sentences. under 280 characters.
- no links in the body; a link, if any, goes in a separate first reply.
- specifics over adjectives: a number, a command, a time, a thing that broke.
- no questions to the reader, except the weekly question post and replies.
